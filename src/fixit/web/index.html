Questions you might have

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You read short snippets and pick the fix from a list. The game teaches you what the lines mean as you go.

How long does a game take?

One sprint is meant to take a few minutes, so you can fit it into a break.

Who is it for?

Students learning Scrum and anyone curious about how software teams work. Instructors can follow how their class plays.

Does the game record what I do?

It logs your game actions and the dialogue you see for the pilot, and it asks for your consent before you play.
